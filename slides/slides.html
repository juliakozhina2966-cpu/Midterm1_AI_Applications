Who should the bank call first?

Predicting credit card default one month ahead

Elisa Ouillet, Iulia Kozhina, Lucas Clodic
FI510E AI & Business Applications, Midterm 1, Rabat Business School

The team can call 10% of its clients. Which ones?

4,500
credit card clients each month
22%
will miss next month's payment
450
calls the collections team can make

Decision: right after the September statement, choose who to call before the October due date (reminder, instalment plan). A wrong call wastes time and annoys a good client.

The data: 30,000 clients, six months of history

What we use

  • Credit limit and education
  • For each month, April to September: repayment status, bill, amount paid
  • 7 ratios we added: share of limit used, share of bills repaid, months late…

What we checked or removed

  • No missing values; undocumented codes grouped
  • Sex, marital status, age removed: not acceptable for a credit decision, costs only 0.002 AUC
  • No leakage: all inputs known before the payment
  • Split 70 / 15 / 15: train, choose, test once

Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository, Default of Credit Card Clients (Yeh & Lien, 2009). Taiwan, 2005, amounts in NT$.

  • 70% of the calls reach a future defaulter, 3.2 times better than chance
  • ROC-AUC 0.782 on the test set vs 0.777 in validation: the model does not overfit
  • The test set was used once, after the model and the rule were fixed

It pays, even if our cost assumptions are wrong

  • Missed defaulter: ~4,700 NT$
    Useless call: 2,000 NT$
  • Value peaks at ~650 calls: 450 is on the profitable part
  • 9 cost scenarios tested: profitable in 9 out of 9
  • Break-even: a call must save only 2.3% of the bill (we assume 10%)

Expected on the test month: 317 defaulters reached and about +1.8 M NT$ (≈ 51,000 €) for 4,500 clients.

The tool: where does one client stand?

Our web page (Flask): enter six months of statements, the model ranks the client among the 4,500 validation clients.

Limits and next steps

  • Old, foreign data: Taiwan, 2005. A Moroccan bank must retrain on its own clients and respect law 09-08 on personal data.
  • Prediction is not prevention: next step, an A/B test. Call half of the top 450, compare default rates.
  • Assumed costs: tested on nine scenarios, to confirm with the finance team.
  • Monitoring: check the hit rate every month and retrain when it drops.
